Why We Show Up

What We Do

Lions Clubs International has one simple call. We serve. In Kilgore that means recognizing young people, supporting our schools, and doing steady work for our neighbors.

Student of the Month

We honor local students who lead, serve, and set the standard for the ones coming up behind them.

Guests & Programs

Every week brings a speaker or guest from the community, from the Rangerettes to local leaders.

Giving Back Locally

The money we raise goes straight back into Kilgore, to the causes and people our members know by name.

Our People

The 2026–2027 Board

Neighbors, business owners, retirees, and volunteers. This is who keeps the lights on.

Ready to join us?

Show up to a Thursday lunch. No commitment needed to come try it out. Bring a friend, bring an appetite, and see whether this is your kind of room. Most of our members walked in exactly the same way.
