Getting a Ticket

How It Works

Tickets are sold in person by Kilgore Lions Club members. They are not sold on this website and they are not sold online anywhere.

  1. Ask any Kilgore Lions Club member. If you know a Lion, that is the quickest way. Members carry tickets with them.
  2. Or fill out the short form below. Tell us your name, your phone number, and how many tickets you want.
  3. A member brings your tickets to you. Someone from the club will call to set up a time and place that works for you.
  4. Pay when you get them. Cash, check, or card, handed over in person. Nothing is charged on this website.
  5. Keep your stub and enjoy the carnival. The drawing is Halloween night at 9:00 PM. You do not need to be there to win.

Have a Member Bring You Tickets

Fill this out and someone from the club will reach out. This form does not charge you anything. It just tells us you want tickets and how to reach you.

If the form does not show up on your phone, open it in a new window, or email info@kilgorelionsclub.org.

100 percent of the proceeds benefit the Kilgore Lions Christmas Project, putting gifts and food in the hands of local families during the holidays.
